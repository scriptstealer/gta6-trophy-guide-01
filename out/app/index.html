⚠ One trophy may be missable — read this first

Positive Mental Attitude (meditate at every spot across Leonida) is the only trophy PowerPyx flags as possibly missable. During Chapter 1, one morning at Jason’s house, Lucia says she is going to meditate and walks out to the balcony. Follow her and accept the optional meditation. It is gone after Chapter 1, and GTA VI has no mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter (15 manual slots plus 1 autosave). The 100% checklist only needs 15 of the 18 spots, so this trophy is the only reason to be careful. Cheats disable trophies and saving — keep them off your platinum run.

Roadmap to the platinum — Vice Got a Price!

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4/10 difficulty at about 200 hours (Push Square says 250+). One playthrough is enough. There are no difficulty trophies and no mission medals. Free roam continues after the story, and nothing on the 100% checklist is missable.

  1. Stage 1 — Story first, Chapters 1–5 about 60–80 hours

    Play the five chapters at your own pace. Along the way: do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation; take optional missions as they appear (100% needs any 100 of several hundred); scan every new vehicle with the WAiNK app (100% needs 200 of 277); study new animals through the binoculars (100% needs 100 of 255); meet Cal in Chapter 1 for the fishing challenge; start the stranger strands as they open — Billy and Bret in Chapter 2, Andy, Randy and Tyrone in Chapter 3, Kenton whenever he returns. Turn on progress and criminal profile notifications in the UI settings so you can see what counts.

    Covers: all 12 story trophies, the 5 stranger-strand finals, Baited My Tackle, Fair Game, Whip It Good, and the Chapter 1 meditation for Positive Mental Attitude.

  2. Stage 2 — The 100% checklist about 100 hours

    After the credits, the checklist opens under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. Progress from the story already counts. PowerPyx calls it lenient — closer to 60% true completion. You need 70 of the 80 challenges, 300 stunts, 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals and 50 of 66 weapons. Only robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests need everything. Work through it and A Long Road follows.

    Covers: A Long Road, Cunning Stunts, The Shootist, Top Fun, Earn Cash, Not Kudos, One Last Thing, Maximum Withdrawal, Pest Control, Dime on the Dollar, Treat Yo Self, Little Boxes, On the Spot, Don’t Touch My Deck, You WAiNKer!, The Goodtime State.

  3. Stage 3 — Strangers & Freaks and cleanup about 10 hours

    Most trophies should be done by now. Strangers & Freaks is not part of 100%, so finish any of the 8 strands still open, then clean up the open-world one-offs: the partner trophies, the airport and tank stunts, the jewelry store, the strength grind, and the five trophies whose methods are still unconfirmed as of launch day. The platinum pops when the last one lands.

    Covers: Strangers & Freaks, Master of None, Stick Fiddler, Hot Together, Retail Therapy, Gunslinger, Catch Me If You Can, Challenging Tank, Sneakthief, A Classic Blitz Play, Let’s Go Bowling, Personal Best, Actions Speak Louder, In The Beginning…, Capital Offense, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There, Ten Pin, and the platinum itself.

No trophies match this filter. There are no online trophies — every one of the 55 is earned in single-player.
